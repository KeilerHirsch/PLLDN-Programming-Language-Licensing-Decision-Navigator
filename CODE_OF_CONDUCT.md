# Community conduct

Discuss evidence and technical trade-offs respectfully.
Harassment, threats, doxxing and discriminatory abuse are not acceptable.
Do not publish another person's private information.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict repeated abuse.
Explain moderation decisions when doing so does not expose private information.
Appeals should identify the decision and relevant context without targeting individuals.
Report sensitive incidents through the private reporting channel in SECURITY.md.
