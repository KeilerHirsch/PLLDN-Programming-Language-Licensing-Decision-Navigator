## The Mission

**An informed engineering decision needs more than a popularity ranking.**

The programming language shapes what you can build and maintain. The licensing terms shape what you can ship. PLLDN puts both questions within reach: explore the options, compare trade-offs and follow the sources behind recorded facts.

**Missing evidence is not a recommendation.** PLLDN keeps exploratory **Preview** guidance separate from its narrower **Reviewed** decision lab. Unassessed catalogue entries remain **UNKNOWN** instead of being dressed up as certainty.
